How It Works

One Button From Your Provider. Everything Else Is Ours.

Your provider taps one of four outcomes on the way out of the room — booked, thinking, price, or timing. That single answer starts a sequence written for that specific objection.

It never opens with a discount, because discounting after hesitation just teaches patients to stall. And it stops the instant she replies, so your team only ever sees warm conversations.
